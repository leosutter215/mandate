The lab

Eight beats, 16 presses end to end. Every beat below opens both your windows on itself, so you can rehearse the back half without sitting through the front of it.

Setting up, in order

  1. 1Plug the smartboard in and set the display to Extend, not Mirror.
  2. 2Open /control on your laptop. This is yours and nobody else sees it.
  3. 3Click Open the screen. Drag that new window onto the smartboard and full-screen it.
  4. 4Every table Chromebook goes to /room and stops there.
  5. 5Press Next on your laptop once. If the smartboard moves, you are set.

When it goes wrong

The smartboard window is showing the wrong thing
It follows the control window and catches up on its own within a quarter of a second. If it is genuinely stuck, reload it. It will land wherever you are, and the room will see a blink.
The two windows will not talk to each other
They have to be the same browser on the same laptop. If it still will not, use /board instead: one window, everything in it, tap the right side to advance.
A Chromebook will not load the questions
Two tables share one. Nothing is counted, nothing is per-person, and nothing later depends on who answered.
The wifi dies
Anything already loaded keeps running. None of these pages talk to a server at any point.
Running short
Cut beat 5, the four buildings, and go from the tower straight to the term chart. Do not cut the money.
