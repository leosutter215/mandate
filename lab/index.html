The lab

Thirteen right-arrow presses, start to finish. Every beat below jumps straight to itself so you can rehearse the back half without sitting through the front.

The three addresses

/mandate/control

Your laptop. The script, the figures, and two buttons. Nobody else sees this.

/mandate/screen

Drag onto the smartboard. Displays only. No controls at all.

/mandate/room

Every table Chromebook. Two questions, nothing else on it.

/mandate/board

Fallback: one window that does everything, if the two-window setup will not cooperate.

/mandate/presenter

The old printed run sheet. Superseded by /control.

When it goes wrong

The laptops are out entirely
Press O. “Change of plan, the computers are out, we’re doing this together.” Both guesses become a show of hands with the script on screen, and the tower gets built on the board by whatever the room votes for. O again switches back. You can do this mid-sentence.
One laptop won’t load
Two tables share. Nothing is counted and nothing is per-person.
A table is on the wrong screen
Small step buttons run along the bottom of their page. Tell them which word to press.
The wifi dies
Anything already loaded keeps running. None of this talks to a server.
Running short
Cut beat 5, go from the tower straight to the slider. Do not cut the money.
