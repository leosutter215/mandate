If something goes wrong

The laptops are not working at all. Press O on the board and say “change of plan, the computers are out, we are doing this together.” The guesses become a show of hands with a script on screen, and the tower gets built on the board by whatever the room votes for. Every other beat is unchanged. You can switch at any point, including halfway through, and switch back with O again.

One laptop will not load it. Two tables share one. Nothing is per-person and nothing is counted.

A table is on the wrong screen. Small step buttons run along the bottom of their page. Tell them which word to press.

The wifi dies mid-session. Anything already loaded keeps running. None of this talks to a server.

Running short. Cut beat 5, go from the tower straight to the slider. Don’t cut the money.

If a teacher asks whether the model is rigged

The slider runs the same engine whatever it is set to, and the only thing it changes is how long an administration lasts. The plan is phased because the budget only frees up as earlier steps start paying for themselves, so a short term never reaches the end of it. The step a four year term never arrives at is the charter amendment, which is the one that makes it permanent. That was not written in. It is what the model does when you shorten the term. Every figure in the money beat is from the Office of Homeless Services budget testimony to City Council on 20 April 2026, and the four programme lines add to the stated total exactly.
